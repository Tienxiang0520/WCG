# Locally bundled sound effects

Author: Kenney / Kenney Vleugels. License: Creative Commons Zero (CC0).
Original samples are unchanged, except `turn-mechanical.ogg`, a layered adaptation of three CC0 samples retained in `sources/turn/`. Its sources and recipe are recorded in `manifest.json` and `licenses/derived-turn.txt`. Playback gains are set in `../battle-audio.js`.
Original license texts are preserved in `licenses/`.

Downloaded from the official asset pages on 2026-10-07:

- Casino Audio 1.1: https://kenney.nl/assets/casino-audio
- Interface Sounds 1.0: https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds
- Impact Sounds 1.0: https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds

`manifest.json` records the selected original filenames, pack, SHA-256 and duration.
No remote asset requests are made during gameplay.
